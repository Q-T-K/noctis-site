Sleep. Train. Recover.

Noctis wakes you at a lighter moment, tells you how recovered you are, and sizes today's training to match. One app for your nights and your days, built for iPhone and Apple Watch. No account. Your health data stays on your devices.

SupportPrivacy Policy

A smarter alarm

Wear your Apple Watch to bed and Noctis watches your heart rate and wrist movement in a 10, 20 or 30-minute window before your alarm, then wakes you with gentle haptics at a lighter moment. Never later than your set time. Your iPhone is the backup; on iOS 26 it rings even in Silent mode or a Focus.

Know how you slept

A sleep score from five parts: duration, regularity, restfulness, sleep stages and timing. Your sleep debt in hours against your own goal, and Week, Month and All Time views.

Know what today can take

Every morning, one readiness score from your heart recovery, last night's sleep and your training load, and one clear call: Push, Train, Maintain, Recover or Rest. Energy, Stress Shield and Strain carry it through the day.

Train at the right load

Exertion from 0 to 10 relative to your own fitness, and Training Status that compares this week with your usual week. Days without your Watch count as unknown, never as rest.

Coach Noctis

One coach on every tab: how long and how hard to train today, when to be in bed tonight, and why. A weekly review every Sunday.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics, no servers. Noctis reads Apple Health with your permission and never writes to it. Everything is calculated on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Smart wake needs an Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. Noctis is a wellness and fitness app, not a medical device.
