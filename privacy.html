Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

The short version: Noctis has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers. Your health data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We don't collect it, see it, sell it, or share it. The only thing that leaves your device is your approximate location, if you allow it, which goes to Apple WeatherKit to fetch the forecast; Noctis never receives or stores it.

Who we are

Noctis Health ("Noctis", "we", "us") is an iPhone and Apple Watch app developed by Quinn Kliman in Victoria, British Columbia, Canada. Questions about this policy can go to support@noctis.health.

Information we collect

None. Noctis has no account system and runs no servers. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

Data the app uses on your device

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Noctis reads health data from Apple Health. That can include sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, walking heart rate average, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, steps, walking/running, cycling and swimming distance, active and resting energy, exercise minutes, workouts, workout effort, VO₂ max, body measurements (weight, body fat, lean mass, BMI), and your date of birth and biological sex (used for heart-rate zones and training load). Noctis uses this data only to calculate your sleep, readiness, energy, strain and stress insights and to time your alarm. Noctis only reads from Apple Health; it does not write any data to it.

HealthKit data is processed and stored only on your devices. It is never sent to us or to any third party. It is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and it is never sold. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Noctis.

Apple Watch

The iPhone and Watch apps sync your alarm settings and overnight sleep data directly with each other through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. During your smart-wake window, the Watch app reads heart rate and motion (and, if you allow it, wrist temperature and blood oxygen from Apple Health) to find a lighter moment to wake you. Noctis does not stage your sleep on the Watch; the sleep stages you see in the app come from Apple Health.

Location (optional)

If you allow it, Noctis uses your approximate location to get local sunrise, sunset, and weather from Apple WeatherKit for sleep and training tips. The location is sent to Apple only to fetch that forecast, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive or store it. Noctis works without location access.

Motion & notifications

Motion data from your Apple Watch is used on-device to find a lighter moment to wake you. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Storage, backup & deletion

App data is stored locally on your devices and may be included in your own iCloud or device backups, under Apple's terms. To delete all Noctis data, delete the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch. Data in Apple Health is managed in the Health app.

Third parties

Noctis shares no data with third parties. The only external service it uses is Apple WeatherKit, as described above.

Children

Noctis is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

Noctis is a wellness app, not a medical device. Its insights are not medical advice and are not meant to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side. Your data is in your hands on your device. You're welcome to contact us with any privacy question, including under Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR, or the CCPA.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
