Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

The short version: Noctis has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers. Your health data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We don't collect it, see it, sell it, or share it. Only two things leave your device on their own, and both go to Apple, never to us: your approximate location, if you allow it, so Apple WeatherKit can fetch the forecast; and, when you open a workout that has a route, points along that route so Apple Maps can draw the map and name the streets. Noctis never receives or stores either on a server. If you turn on calendar sync, the workouts you plan become events in a calendar you choose, which syncs through that calendar's own account, never through us. If you choose to email us, we receive what you send.

Who we are

Noctis Health ("Noctis", "we", "us") is an iPhone and Apple Watch app developed by Quinn Kliman in Victoria, British Columbia, Canada. Questions about this policy can go to support@noctis.health.

Information we collect

None. Noctis has no account system and runs no servers. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software. Nothing is sent to us unless you email us (see "If you contact us" below).

Data the app uses on your device

If you contact us

If you send feedback from Settings → Profile → Help & about → Send feedback, the email opens in your own mail app and you see everything before you send it. We then receive your email address, your message, the app, iOS and watchOS versions and your iPhone and Apple Watch models, plus anything you choose to attach: a screenshot, or last night's log (alarm and Watch timings with that night's heart rate and sleep stages). Both attachments are off unless you turn them on. We use what you send only to answer you and fix problems, never for advertising, and we delete it when it's no longer needed for that. The same applies if you email support@noctis.health directly.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Noctis reads health data from Apple Health. That can include sleep (including sleep stages), heart rate, resting heart rate, walking heart rate average, heart rate variability and the heartbeat timing behind it, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, steps, active energy, exercise minutes, walking/running, cycling, swimming and other sports' distances and speeds, workouts and their routes, workout effort, heart-rate recovery, running power, speed and form (stride length, ground contact, vertical oscillation), cycling power, speed, cadence and FTP, swim strokes and water temperature, resting energy, VO₂ max, body measurements (weight, body fat, lean mass, BMI), and your date of birth and biological sex (used for heart-rate zones and training load). Noctis uses this data only to calculate your sleep, readiness, energy, stress, exertion and training-load insights and your workout pages, to shape Coach Noctis suggestions, and to time your alarm. Noctis only reads from Apple Health; it does not write any data to it.

HealthKit data is processed and stored only on your devices. Noctis never sends it to us or to any third party (the route points Apple Maps needs to show a workout's map stay with Apple, see below); the only way any of it reaches us is if you choose to attach last night's log to an email (see below). It is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and it is never sold. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Noctis.

Workout routes & maps

When you open a workout that has a route, Noctis shows it on Apple Maps. To draw the map and name the streets it passed (for a title like "Dallas Road Loop"), the app sends map requests and a few points along the route to Apple's map and geocoding services, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Only coordinates are sent, with nothing that identifies you. We never receive them. The street names and map pictures are kept on your device. Indoor workouts and workouts without a route send nothing.

Apple Watch

The iPhone and Watch apps sync your alarm settings and overnight sleep data directly with each other through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. During your smart-wake window, the Watch app reads heart rate and motion (and, if you allow it, wrist temperature and blood oxygen from Apple Health) to find a lighter moment to wake you. Noctis does not stage your sleep on the Watch; the sleep stages you see in the app come from Apple Health.

Location (optional)

If you allow it, Noctis uses your approximate location to get local sunrise, sunset, and weather from Apple WeatherKit for sleep and training tips. The location is sent to Apple only to fetch that forecast, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive or store it. Noctis works without location access.

Calendar (optional)

If you turn on Settings → Fitness → Planned workouts → Add to calendar and allow calendar access, Noctis adds each workout you plan to a calendar you pick (or a new "Noctis" calendar): its sport, time, the session's steps, one alert and a link back to the app. When you change, finish, skip or delete a plan, Noctis updates or removes its own event. iOS only lets an app update its events later with full calendar access, which technically allows reading your calendars. Noctis uses it only to list your calendars so you can pick one, and to find its own events again around a plan's day. It never reads, copies or sends your other events. Events are kept by your calendar app and sync through the account that calendar belongs to (for example iCloud, Google or Exchange), under that provider's terms. We never receive them. You can turn this off in the same setting, and revoke access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Motion & notifications

Motion data from your Apple Watch is used on-device to find a lighter moment to wake you. Notifications, including reminders for the workouts you plan, are scheduled locally on your device.

Storage, backup & deletion

App data is stored locally on your devices and may be included in your own iCloud or device backups, under Apple's terms. Noctis's widgets and Watch complication read a small summary that the app keeps on the same device. Workout details read from Apple Health (routes, heart rate and other readings) are cached on the device to open workouts quickly and are left out of backups. You can clear your profile in Settings → Profile → Data & privacy. To delete all Noctis data, delete the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch. Data in Apple Health is managed in the Health app.

Third parties

Noctis shares no data with third parties. The only external services it uses are Apple WeatherKit and Apple Maps, as described above. If you turn on calendar sync, your own calendar account stores the workout events, as described under Calendar.

Children

Noctis is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any information from children.

Medical disclaimer

Noctis is a wellness and fitness app, not a medical device. Its insights are not medical advice and are not meant to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side. Your data is in your hands on your device. You're welcome to contact us with any privacy question, including under Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR, or the CCPA.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
