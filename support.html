Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@noctis.health. We usually reply within 1–2 business days. Tell us your iPhone and Apple Watch models, their iOS/watchOS versions, and the Noctis version (Settings → About).

Frequently asked questions

My scores are missing or say "Building your baseline"

Noctis learns your personal normal ranges, which takes about a week (7 nights) of sleep data from Apple Health. Until then, readiness shows "Building your baseline" with a nights counter. Some personalization, such as your sleep profile, keeps refining over roughly 14 nights. Make sure Noctis has access in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Noctis.

How does the smart alarm work?

Wear your Apple Watch to bed with the Noctis Watch app installed. Within your smart-wake window, Noctis watches your heart rate and movement for a lighter moment and wakes you then. At your set alarm time at the latest, the alarm always rings, on the Watch with an iPhone backup.

The alarm didn't ring on my Watch

Check that the Watch is charged (30% or more is recommended) and on your wrist, that the Noctis Watch app is installed, and that notifications for Noctis are allowed on both devices. Opening Noctis on your iPhone before bed makes sure tonight's alarm has synced.

Why does Noctis want my location?

Location is optional. It's only used to fetch local sunrise, sunset and weather for better tips. It is sent to Apple WeatherKit only to get the forecast; Noctis never receives or stores it. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, and your health data never leaves your devices.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch. Health data is managed in the Health app.

Noctis is a wellness app and doesn't provide medical advice.
