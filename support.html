Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@noctis.health. We usually reply within 1–2 business days. Tell us your iPhone and Apple Watch models, their iOS/watchOS versions, and the Noctis version (Settings → Profile → Help & about, at the bottom).

Frequently asked questions

My scores are missing or say "Building your baseline"

Noctis learns your personal normal ranges, which takes about a week (7 nights) of sleep data from Apple Health. Until then, readiness shows "Building your baseline" with a nights counter. Some personalization, such as your sleep profile, keeps refining over roughly 14 nights. Make sure Noctis has access in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Noctis.

Can I try Noctis before I have much data?

Yes. Turn on Settings → Profile → Help & about → Sample data, and every screen fills with a realistic example. Turn it off to go back to your own data. Sample data never changes anything in Apple Health or in your own history.

How does the smart alarm work?

Set your wake-up time in Settings → Sleep → Alarm: one time for every day, weekdays and weekend, or a different time each day. Wear your Apple Watch to bed with the Noctis Watch app installed. In your smart-wake window (10, 20 or 30 minutes, or Off), the Watch watches your heart rate and wrist movement for a lighter moment and wakes you with haptics then. At your wake-up time at the latest, the alarm always rings. Your iPhone is the backup: on iOS 26 it rings even in Silent mode or a Focus (allow Alarms when asked); on iOS 17–25 it uses time-sensitive notifications. Without an Apple Watch, the iPhone alarm rings at your set time and smart wake is hidden.

The alarm didn't ring on my Watch

Check that the Watch is charged (30% or more is recommended) and on your wrist, that the Noctis Watch app is installed, and that notifications for Noctis are allowed on both devices. Opening Noctis on your iPhone before bed makes sure tonight's alarm has synced.

Why does Noctis want my location?

Location is optional. It's only used to fetch local sunrise, sunset and weather for better tips. It is sent to Apple WeatherKit only to get the forecast; Noctis never receives or stores it. You can turn it on or off in Settings → Profile → Data & privacy. See the Privacy Policy.

My history looks wrong after changing Health sources

Use Settings → Profile → Data & privacy → Re-import from Health. Noctis reads all your sleep and workouts from Apple Health again and rebuilds its history. It changes nothing until everything has been read, and it deletes nothing.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, and your health data never leaves your devices.

How do I delete my data?

To clear your profile (name, birth month, sex, goal and training settings), use Settings → Profile → Data & privacy → Clear profile. To delete everything, delete the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch. Health data is managed in the Health app.

Noctis is a wellness and fitness app, not a medical device, and doesn't provide medical advice.
